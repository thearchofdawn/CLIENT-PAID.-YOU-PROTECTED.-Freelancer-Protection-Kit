STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you
decide.

What do I receive?

The main guide and worksheets, onboarding templates, a standalone HTML payment tracker, invoice and payment admin documents, and agreement and clause resources.

Is this designed for Indian freelancers?

Yes. The kit is designed around common Indian freelance workflows. Adapt the documents to your own work, client, and applicable local requirements.

Can I edit the templates?

The onboarding, invoice/admin and agreement packs include editable DOCX files and printable PDFs. The tracker is a standalone HTML tool; its PDF guide is also included. The ebook and worksheets are supplied as PDFs.

Is the payment tracker an Excel file or a web app?

It is a standalone HTML tool that opens in a current browser. It can run locally without a hosted account or build step; it is not an Excel workbook.

Does the tracker upload my data to the cloud?

The supplied implementation stores records in browser-local storage on that device and makes no server/API calls. It does not sync across browsers or devices, does not send reminders, and does not verify payments. Export backups regularly; do not store payment secrets.

Can I use these templates with different freelance services?

They are adaptable starting points for common client-service projects. Replace every placeholder and adapt scope, price, dates, rights and processes to the actual engagement.

Are the agreement templates legal advice?

No. They are educational starting-point templates, not legal advice or lawyer-reviewed agreements. They do not guarantee enforceability. Consult a qualified lawyer for important or complex engagements.

Does buying this guarantee that clients will pay on time?

No. No template or toolkit can guarantee a client's behaviour, collect a debt for you, or prevent disputes. These resources support clearer expectations, communication and recordkeeping.

How do I receive my files?

After checkout, follow the delivery instructions shown by the seller. If a file or access link does not arrive, contact the support email below.

What if I have trouble accessing a download?

Contact thearchofdawn@gmail.com with your order details. Please do not send card details or passwords.
